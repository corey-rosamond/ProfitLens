## 🧪 STEP 7: Test Your GPT

### Click "Preview" (right side) and test these:

#### Test 1: Basic Calculation
**Input:** "Calculate FBA fees for a product: $29.99, 1.5 lbs, 10x8x3 inches, Home & Kitchen"

**Expected:** Should show exact fees with breakdown

#### Test 2: BSR Analysis
**Input:** "The product has BSR 8,543 in Toys & Games"

**Expected:** Should show sales estimate with ±25% range and velocity indicator

#### Test 3: Quick Mode
**Input:** "Quick: $30, 2lbs, Sports, BSR 4000"

**Expected:** Should give one-line analysis with score and GO/NO-GO

#### Test 4: Portfolio
**Input:** "Analyze these: yoga mat $25 2lbs BSR 5000, water bottle $18 1lb BSR 8000"
