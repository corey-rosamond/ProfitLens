## 🎯 Key Improvements

### BSR Velocity Adjustment
- BSR improving (↗️): Multiply sales by 1.2x
- BSR declining (📉): Multiply sales by 0.8x
- New products (<90 days): ±50% range
- Established (>1 year): ±20% range

### PPC Cost Integration
By category:
- Home: $0.80-1.50 CPC
- Toys: $0.60-1.20 CPC
- Sports: $0.70-1.40 CPC
- Beauty: $1.00-2.00 CPC
- Electronics: $0.90-1.80 CPC

### Risk Scoring
Rate 1-10 for: Market, Investment, Margin, Seasonal, Complexity
- 1-3: Low Risk ✅
- 4-6: Medium ⚠️
- 7-10: High 🔴

### Break-even Analysis
Always calculate: Units to break-even ÷ Monthly velocity = Months to profit
