# FBA Calculation Examples

## Example 1: Kitchen Gadget (Standard Size)

### Product Details:
- **Product**: Silicone Spatula Set
- **Selling Price**: $24.99
- **Product Cost**: $7.50
- **Category**: Home & Kitchen
- **Weight**: 0.8 lbs (12.8 oz)
- **Dimensions**: 11" x 3" x 1.5"

### Step-by-Step Calculation:

1. **Referral Fee**:
   - Home & Kitchen = 15%
   - $24.99 × 0.15 = $3.75

2. **FBA Fulfillment Fee**:
   - Weight: 12.8 oz (Small Standard)
   - Fee tier: 8-12 oz = $3.58

3. **Monthly Storage Fee**:
   - Volume: 11 × 3 × 1.5 = 49.5 cubic inches = 0.029 cubic feet
   - Jan-Sep: $0.029 × $0.87 = $0.03
   - Oct-Dec: $0.029 × $2.40 = $0.07

4. **Total Fees**: $3.75 + $3.58 + $0.03 = $7.36

5. **Net Profit**: $24.99 - $7.50 - $7.36 = $10.13

6. **Profit Margin**: ($10.13 ÷ $24.99) × 100 = 40.5%

7. **ROI**: ($10.13 ÷ $7.50) × 100 = 135.1%

**Verdict**: HIGHLY PROFITABLE ✅

## Key Patterns to Remember:

1. **Products under $15**: Often unprofitable due to fixed FBA fees
2. **Sweet spot**: $20-50 products with <1 lb weight
3. **Heavy items**: Need higher prices to offset shipping
4. **Special categories**: Watch for higher referral fees (Jewelry 20%, Clothing 17%)
5. **Grocery trap**: Items under $15 pay 15% instead of 8%
6. **Storage impact**: Minimal for small items but significant for large/slow-moving items

## Quick Profitability Check:

### Minimum Viable Pricing Formula:
```
Minimum Price = (Product Cost + Desired Profit) ÷ (1 - Total Fee %)

Example: $8 cost, want $5 profit, 15% referral + ~$3.50 FBA
Minimum Price = ($8 + $5 + $3.50) ÷ 0.85 = $19.41
```

### Rule of Thumb:
- Selling price should be **3x your product cost** for healthy margins
- If you can't achieve 25% margin, reconsider the product
