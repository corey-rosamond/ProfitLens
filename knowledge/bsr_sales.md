# BSR to Sales Estimation Guide

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

**These are ESTIMATES based on aggregated industry data, NOT real-time information.**
- Accuracy: ±30% variance is common
- Source: Historical seller reports and tool aggregations
- Age of data: Compiled from 2023-2024 observations
- Real sales can vary significantly based on:
  - Recent algorithm changes
  - Seasonal fluctuations
  - Promotional activities
  - Competition changes

**For real-time data**: Integration with SellerSprite API or similar services required.

## 📊 How BSR Works

**BSR (Best Sellers Rank)** indicates a product's sales performance relative to others in its category. Lower numbers = more sales.

### Important Notes:
- BSR updates hourly based on recent sales
- Each category has its own BSR scale
- Subcategory BSR ≠ Main category BSR
- Seasonal items have volatile BSRs
- New products may not have BSR yet

## 🎯 Sales Velocity Indicators

### High Velocity (Fast-Moving):
- BSR < 1,000
- 500+ sales/month
- Daily sales consistency
- Good for cash flow

### Medium Velocity:
- BSR 1,000-10,000
- 100-500 sales/month
- Weekly sales patterns
- Balanced inventory needed

### Low Velocity (Slow-Moving):
- BSR > 10,000
- <100 sales/month
- Sporadic sales
- Risk of long-term storage fees

## 💡 Important Considerations

### BSR Volatility:
- **New Products**: BSR can swing wildly in first 30 days
- **Seasonal Items**: BSR changes dramatically by season
- **Out of Stock**: BSR deteriorates quickly when unavailable
- **Lightning Deals**: Temporary BSR boost during promotions

### Confidence Levels Explained:
- **High**: Reliable estimates based on extensive data
- **Good**: Solid estimates with minor variations
- **Moderate**: General range, actual may vary ±30%
- **Low**: Rough estimate, high uncertainty
- **Very Low**: Minimal data, use with caution

## 📊 Quick Reference Formula

### Rough Estimation (All Categories):
```
Monthly Sales ≈ 100,000 ÷ BSR (very rough estimate)

Examples:
- BSR 1,000 ≈ 100 sales/month
- BSR 5,000 ≈ 20 sales/month
- BSR 10,000 ≈ 10 sales/month
```

### Market Size Assessment:
- **Large Market**: Top 100 BSR < 500
- **Medium Market**: Top 100 BSR 500-2,000
- **Small Market**: Top 100 BSR > 2,000
- **Niche Market**: Top 10 BSR > 5,000

## 🚨 Red Flags:
- BSR > 100,000 in main category
- Highly volatile BSR (changes >50% daily)
- No BSR after 30 days live
- Subcategory BSR only (no main category)
