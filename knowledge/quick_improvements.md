## 🚀 BSR Velocity Adjustments

### Dynamic Sales Estimation Based on Trend
When analyzing BSR, also consider trajectory:

**BSR Improving (Getting Lower)**
- Product gaining momentum
- Multiply sales estimate by 1.2x
- Note: "📈 Trending up - sales likely higher than base estimate"

**BSR Declining (Getting Higher)**
- Product losing momentum
- Multiply sales estimate by 0.8x
- Note: "📉 Trending down - sales may be lower than estimate"

**BSR Stable**
- Use standard estimates
- Note: "➡️ Stable sales pattern"

### New vs Established Products
**New Products (<90 days old)**
- BSR highly volatile
- Provide wider range (±50%)
- Note: "🆕 New product - BSR still stabilizing"

**Established Products (>1 year)**
- BSR more reliable
- Tighter range (±20%)
- Note: "✅ Established product - more reliable estimates"

## 📊 Break-Even Analysis

### Quick Break-Even Calculator
```
Break-Even Units = Initial Investment ÷ Profit per Unit

Example:
Investment: $3,000
Profit/unit: $8
Break-even: 375 units

At 300 units/month = 1.25 months to break-even
```

### Time to Profitability
```
Month 1: -$Investment (inventory + launch)
Month 2: Revenue - Costs - PPC
Month 3: Approaching break-even
Month 4+: Profitable

Include in analysis:
"📈 Break-even: ~375 units (1.5 months at current velocity)"
```
