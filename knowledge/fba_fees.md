## 📐 Size Tier Classifications

### Small Standard
- Max dimensions: 15" x 12" x 0.75"
- Max weight: 16 oz

### Large Standard
- Max dimensions: 18" x 14" x 8"
- Max weight: 20 lb

### Oversize Determination
Length + Girth (2x width + 2x height) calculation:
- Small Oversize: ≤130" and ≤70 lb
- Medium Oversize: ≤130" and ≤150 lb
- Large Oversize: ≤165" and ≤150 lb
- Special Oversize: Exceeds large oversize limits

## 💡 Quick Calculation Formulas

### Total Amazon Fees
```
Total Fees = Referral Fee + FBA Fulfillment Fee + Monthly Storage Fee
```

### Net Profit
```
Net Profit = Selling Price - Product Cost - Total Amazon Fees
```

### Profit Margin
```
Profit Margin % = (Net Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
```

### ROI (Return on Investment)
```
ROI % = (Net Profit ÷ Product Cost) × 100
```

### Break-Even Units
```
Break-Even = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price - Variable Costs per Unit)
```

## 🎯 Profitability Guidelines

- **Excellent**: >40% profit margin

## 📝 Important Notes

1. **Always verify** current fees as Amazon updates them periodically
2. **Additional fees** may apply for special handling, returns, or removal orders
3. **Peak season** (Oct-Dec) has higher storage fees
4. **Category approval** may be required for restricted categories
5. **PPC costs** are not included in these calculations but typically run 10-30% of revenue
