# ProfitLens Transparency Commitment

## Our Promise: No Fake Data, No False Precision

We believe honesty builds trust. Here's exactly what our GPT provides and where it comes from:

## ✅ What We Know EXACTLY

### Amazon FBA Fees
- **Source**: Amazon's official 2024 fee schedule
- **Accuracy**: 100% for published rates
- **Updated**: Quarterly with Amazon changes
- **Confidence**: HIGH - These are exact

## 📊 What We ESTIMATE

### BSR to Sales Conversions
- **Source**: Aggregated industry reports (2023-2024)
- **Accuracy**: ±30% typical variance
- **Method**: Historical pattern analysis
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM - General ranges only
- **Better Option**: SellerSprite API for real-time data

### Competition Levels
- **Source**: Seller experience and observations
- **Accuracy**: Heuristic guidelines
- **Method**: Pattern recognition
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM - Typical patterns
- **Better Option**: Manual Amazon research

### Seasonal Patterns
- **Source**: Historical category trends
- **Accuracy**: Category averages only
- **Method**: Past 2-year aggregation
- **Confidence**: LOW-MEDIUM - Varies by product
- **Better Option**: Product-specific history

### Opportunity Scores
- **Source**: Our analytical model
- **Accuracy**: Framework, not prediction
- **Method**: Weighted 5-factor analysis
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM - Relative comparison tool
- **Better Option**: Comprehensive market research

## 🚫 What We DON'T Do

We will NEVER:
- Claim estimates are exact
- Hide our data sources
- Pretend to have real-time data when we don't
- Make up numbers
- Present patterns as guarantees

## 💬 How We Communicate This

Every analysis clearly states:

### High Confidence (Exact Data) 🟢
"This fee is $X.XX based on Amazon's published rates"

### Medium Confidence (Estimates) 🟡
"Based on industry estimates, sales are approximately X-X units (±30% variance typical)"

### Low Confidence (Patterns) 🔴
"Historical patterns suggest X, but individual products vary significantly"

## 📋 User Rights

Users have the right to know:
1. Where every number comes from
2. How confident we are in each metric
3. What better alternatives exist
4. When data was last updated
5. What assumptions we're making

## 🤝 Our Commitment

We commit to:
- **Transparency** over false precision
- **Honesty** about limitations
- **Education** about data sources
- **Continuous** improvement
- **User empowerment** through information

## 💡 Philosophy

> "We'd rather give you an honest range than a false specific number. We'd rather admit uncertainty than pretend certainty. We'd rather educate about limitations than hide them."

## 📊 Tracking Our Transparency

We measure success by:
- User trust scores (not just accuracy claims)
- Clarity of communication
- Reduced user mistakes from overconfidence
- Informed decision-making
- Long-term user retention through trust
